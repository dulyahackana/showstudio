# Sample assets

Everything in `samples/` is either generated here or a freely licensed model or recording.
Models are prepared by `scripts/prepare-assets.ts`: real-world scale, standing on the floor, unused
prop variants and baked animations removed (shows pose the joints themselves), and the Kenney kit
made matte. Sounds are cut by `scripts/prepare-audio.ts`. Every third-party model and sound effect
is **CC0 1.0**, and the music is public domain; no attribution is required, but credit is given here.

The models are generic fantasy assets. The attraction's look nods to the dragon-on-a-hoard genre,
but it uses no characters, names or likenesses from any franchise.

All sounds are mono 44.1 kHz MP3 at -16 LUFS; each show cue sets its own level, and every speaker zone enforces its ceiling.

To rebuild after changing scales, props or cuts:

```bash
bun scripts/prepare-assets.ts <folder with the downloaded models>
bun scripts/prepare-audio.ts <folder with the downloaded sounds>   # needs ffmpeg
bun run gen:samples
```
